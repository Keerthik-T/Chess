Game Information

A serverless, peer-to-peer and offline chess application inspired by Furina.

  • Online P2P: Share links via WebRTC PeerJS with no server.
  • Pass & Play: Play on the same screen offline with auto-flip.
  • Bluetooth: Connect to BLE smart boards wirelessly.
  • Dual Window: Two tabs sync offline on the same computer.
